How it works

From "let's play" to first round in 30 seconds.

No cards to shuffle, no rules to read aloud, no one hunting for the app store password.

  1. Host opens a room

    On any phone or laptop. Cast it to the TV for maximum drama. Pick a topic pack and you're set.

  2. Friends scan to join

    Scan the QR code or type the 4-letter room code. It opens in the browser. Nobody installs anything.

  3. Bluff, argue, accuse

    Everyone gets the secret word, except the liar. One-word clues, a heated debate, then the vote.

FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

Do my friends need to download an app or make an account?

No. Guests scan the QR code on the host's screen (or enter the 4-letter room code) and play in their phone's web browser. No downloads, no sign-up, no email.

How many people can play?

3 to 12 players. It's punchiest with 5–8, and gloriously chaotic at 12.

How does a round work?

Everyone sees a 16-word topic grid with the secret word highlighted, except the liar, who sees only the grid. Each player gives a one-word clue, then you discuss and vote. If the liar is caught, they get one last guess at the word. Name it and they still win.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

The full game with 45 topics is free forever, with no ads and no time limits. If you host a lot and want more variety, you can unlock premium packs or the Party Pass. Only the host needs to unlock; everyone else plays free.

Can we play remotely?

Yes. It's designed for being in the same room, but if the host shares their screen on a video call, remote players can join with the room code on their own devices.

Is it family-friendly?

Yes, by default. The free packs and Family Night are suitable for all ages. The After Dark pack is 18+ and only appears when the host chooses it.

How do I unlock a pack after buying it?

You'll get a code by email right after checkout. Enter it in the host settings ("Have a code?") and your packs unlock on that device. Lost it? Use "Restore purchase" with the email you paid with.

What do you do with my data?

As little as possible. No accounts, no ad trackers, and we use cookie-free, privacy-friendly analytics. Player names and clues exist only for the length of your game.
